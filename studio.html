A quick field guide.

Open an image or use the included alpine illustration. Pick a look, then make it your own.

In Mask & protect, paint the areas you want to preserve. Color region selects connected pixels with similar color; it is not AI segmentation. Hold Shift to subtract.

B Brush · E Erase · W Region · R Rectangle
Ctrl / ⌘ Z Undo mask · Shift Ctrl / ⌘ Z Redo
Space Hold to see original

In Compare view, drag across the image to move the divider. Mask tools work in ASCII view.
